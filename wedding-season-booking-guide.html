When to Book Your Wedding Outfit: A Custom-Stitching Timeline

Custom stitching runs on real-world timelines — fabric sourcing, embroidery, and fittings all take actual time. Here's a realistic guide to when you should start, by outfit type.

🪔 3+ Months Out

Bridal lehengas & heavily embroidered outfits
  • Heavy zardozi, hand embroidery, and stonework take real time — often several weeks of hand-work alone, before stitching even begins.
  • Bridal fabric (especially imported or specialty pieces) sometimes needs to be sourced specifically, which isn't instant.
  • Bridal fittings usually need 2–3 rounds to get right, spaced out so the outfit can be adjusted and rechecked properly — not rushed into one sitting.
  • This window also protects you if your wedding falls in peak season (Oct–Feb), when every boutique's order book fills up fastest.

🎉 6–8 Weeks Out

Sangeet, reception, mehendi & haldi outfits
  • Enough time for a moderately worked outfit — sequins, thread work, gota patti — without the multi-week hand-embroidery timeline of a bridal piece.
  • Still comfortably allows 2 fitting rounds if needed.
  • This is also a good window to finalize your AI Design Studio concept and get fabric matched against actual stock before anything is cut.

👗 3–4 Weeks Out

Guest outfits & simpler designs
  • Comfortable for straightforward stitching — plain or lightly worked kurtis, anarkalis, simple gowns — with one fitting round.
  • Fabric that's already in stock moves faster than anything needing sourcing, so flexibility on fabric choice helps at this stage.

⏱️ 1–2 Weeks Out

Rush orders — possible, but limited
  • We can sometimes accommodate this, but only for simple designs, only with fabric already on our shelves, and usually with just one fitting.
  • Heavy embroidery or bridal work isn't realistic in this window — not because we won't try, but because the hand-work genuinely can't be compressed without quality suffering.
  • If you're this close to your date, call ahead before starting a design so we can tell you honestly what's actually possible.
Worth knowing: October–February is wedding season in North India, and order books at every custom boutique — ours included — fill up fastest in this window. If your event falls here, booking at the earlier end of each range above gives you real breathing room instead of a tight squeeze.
