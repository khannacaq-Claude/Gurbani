Our Story

Gurbani Fashion started as a small family boutique in Faridabad with one simple idea: your outfit should feel like it was made for exactly you — because it was. What began with a needle, thread, and a lot of care has grown into something a little unusual for a neighborhood boutique: an AI Design Studio that turns your own words and photos into real design concepts, still finished by hand by our own tailors.

We haven't changed why we started — helping people feel their best on the days that matter most. We've just found new ways to do it.

— Team Gurbani Fashion

Privacy Notice

What We Collect

Name, phone number, email, photos you choose to upload (selfies, fabric samples, lace/accessory references, inspiration images), body measurements (if provided), and design descriptions/preferences you type.

Why We Collect It

To generate AI-powered design concepts tailored to your request, provide personalized style suggestions, process and fulfill orders, and improve our recommendations over time.

How Your Photos Are Used

Fabric, lace, accessory, and inspiration photos: used to generate AI design images reflecting your actual materials, sent to our AI image partner (Replicate) for this purpose only.

Selfies: used only to generate a written personalized style analysis (e.g. suggestions on necklines, colors, and fits). Selfies are never used to generate design images and are never shared with our image-generation provider.

Who We Share Data With

Anthropic (Claude AI) — processes text and photos to generate design concepts and style analysis.
Replicate — processes design descriptions and reference photos (excluding selfies) to generate preview images.
We do not sell your data to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Who Owns the Generated Designs

Replicate's Terms of Service and Black Forest Labs' terms for the FLUX model we use both confirm that neither of them claims any ownership over the images generated through our AI Design Studio — those rights pass to us. We keep a dated record of the exact terms in effect when we rely on them, in case it's ever needed to settle a question about a design's ownership. As between us and you, see our Terms of Service for how that's handled.

Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data, request correction, or request deletion at any time by contacting us at 9899810977 or khannacaq@gmail.com. We'll respond within 7 business days.

Security

Data is stored on MongoDB Atlas (Mumbai region) with access restricted to authorized Gurbani Fashion administrators. Body measurements specifically are protected behind an additional password inside our admin system, separate from regular order and customer management access — so measurements aren't visible to just any staff login. We do not store payment card details — payments are processed via Razorpay directly.
