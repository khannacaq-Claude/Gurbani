Blouse Designs & Neckline Styles for Sarees & Lehengas

The blouse is often the most customized part of an outfit — and the one people struggle most to describe. Here's a plain-language rundown of neckline, sleeve, and back styles to help you know what to ask for.

Neckline Styles

Sweetheart Neck

A curved, heart-shaped dip at the center front — softens the neckline without being too deep. Works well for most body types and occasions.

BEST FOR: receptions, parties, most saree blouses

Boat Neck (Bateau)

A wide, shallow neckline that runs almost horizontally from shoulder to shoulder. Elegant and structured — a classic choice for silk sarees and formal occasions.

BEST FOR: silk sarees, formal/office-adjacent festive wear

Deep V-Neck

A sharp V cut that goes deeper than a standard round neck — modern and a little bold. Works best paired with the right inner lining/support for comfort.

BEST FOR: parties, receptions, contemporary lehengas

Round / Collar Neck

A simple, modest round cut, sometimes finished with a small stand collar. Comfortable and versatile, especially for pooja and daytime functions.

BEST FOR: pooja functions, daytime festive wear, everyday kurtis

Halter Neck

Straps that tie or fasten behind the neck rather than over the shoulders, often paired with a backless or low-back design. Striking and modern.

BEST FOR: sangeet, reception, evening parties

High Neck

Sits close to the base of the neck, often paired with full or 3/4th sleeves. A more covered, structured look that still reads as festive when paired with embellishment.

BEST FOR: winter functions, more traditional or modest preferences

Sleeve Styles

Sleeveless

Clean and versatile — puts more visual focus on the neckline and embellishment. The most common default for lehenga blouses.

Cap Sleeve

A short sleeve that just covers the top of the shoulder — a small amount of coverage without the weight of a full sleeve.

3/4th or Full (Bell) Sleeve

More coverage and often more embellishment surface — bell sleeves in particular add a flowy, dramatic silhouette. A popular choice for anarkalis and winter functions.

Back Designs

Deep-Back / Backless

A low back cut, sometimes finished with a single string or tassel closure. Common for reception and sangeet blouses where the back becomes a design feature in its own right.

Tasseled or Knotted Back

Ties, tassels, or a decorative knot at the back — turns the closure itself into a design element rather than hiding it.

Closed / Full Back

A more covered back, usually with a zip or hook closure — the practical, comfortable default when the back isn't meant to be a focal point.
