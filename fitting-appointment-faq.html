What to Expect at Your Fitting Appointment

First time getting something custom-stitched? Here's a straight answer to the questions people usually have before their first fitting.

Before You Come In

What should I wear to a fitting?

Wear well-fitted clothing rather than loose layers — it makes it easier to check how the garment sits against your body, and easier for us to pin or mark any adjustments accurately.

Do I need to book in advance, or can I walk in?

Booking ahead through your account (under Appointments) guarantees you a slot and lets us prep your order and measurements before you arrive. Walk-ins are welcome when we have availability, but booking avoids any wait.

Can someone else be fitted on my behalf?

The measurements need to be taken on the actual person who'll wear the outfit — fit can't be reliably transferred from someone else's body, even a similar size. If you can't come in person, our Self-Measurement Guide walks through taking accurate measurements at home instead.

During the Appointment

How long does a fitting appointment take?

A straightforward fitting usually takes 20–30 minutes. Bridal or heavily detailed outfits can take longer, since there's more to check — drape, embellishment placement, and overall balance, not just length and width.

How many fitting rounds will I need?

Most outfits need just one fitting near the end of stitching. Bridal and heavily embroidered pieces usually need 2–3 rounds, spaced out so adjustments can actually be made and rechecked properly rather than rushed.

What happens if something doesn't fit right?

That's exactly what the fitting appointment is for — we mark and adjust it on the spot where possible, or take it back for the tailoring team to correct before your next visit or delivery. Flagging it at the fitting, rather than after you've taken it home, is always the easiest way to fix it.

After Your Fitting

Can I track where my order is after the fitting?

Yes — sign in and check the Order & Fitting Journey tracker to see exactly where things stand, from stitching through to ready-for-pickup.

What if I want to change something after seeing the fitted piece?

Small adjustments (length, fit, minor placement) are a normal part of the fitting process. Larger design changes at this stage may affect timeline or cost, since fabric may already be cut — it's always worth flagging preferences as early as possible, ideally at the design stage.

Worth knowing: the earlier and more specifically you flag a concern — at the fitting, not after you've left with the outfit — the easier and faster it is for us to get it right.
