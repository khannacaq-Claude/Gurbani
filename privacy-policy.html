Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Gurbani Fashion Boutique ("we", "us", "our") operates gurbanifashion.com. This page explains what personal information we collect, why, and how it's handled. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and profile photo, provided via Google Sign-In.
  • Contact details: phone number, if you provide one for order updates, invoices, or measurements.
  • Order and measurement information: garment measurements, design preferences, order history, and delivery details you provide for stitching services.
  • Photos: reference photos (selfies, fabric, accessories, inspiration images) you choose to upload to the AI Design Studio, only with your explicit consent at the time of upload. If you upload a selfie or any photo of a person, that consent includes confirming it's a genuine photo of yourself and that you have the right to use it.
  • Payment information: we do not store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves — payments are processed directly by Razorpay and Paytm, whose own privacy policies govern that data.
  • Usage data: basic technical information (browser type, pages visited) that helps us keep the site working properly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your stitching orders, generate invoices, and communicate with you about them.
  • To generate AI design concepts based on your description and any photos you've chosen to upload.
  • To personalize your experience (e.g. pre-filling your saved measurements for future orders).
  • To improve the site and the services we offer.

3. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share the minimum necessary data with:

  • Google — for sign-in authentication.
  • Anthropic — processes your description and any uploaded photos (including selfies) to generate design concepts and a personalized style write-up.
  • Replicate — generates preview images from your description and reference photos. Selfies specifically are never sent to Replicate and never used to generate a design image.
  • Razorpay and Paytm — to process payments securely. We never see or store your full payment credentials.
📸 On uploaded photos specifically: you control what you upload, and it's used only to generate your personalized design concepts. We do not use your photos for marketing or share them publicly without asking you first. Selfies specifically are analyzed once for style suggestions and then discarded — the photo itself is not stored; only the resulting written analysis is kept.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account, order, and invoice information for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet our tax and accounting obligations under Indian law. Fabric, lace, accessory, and inspiration photos you upload are retained as part of your design record. Selfies are the one exception: the raw photo is discarded immediately after the one-time style analysis it's used for, and is not retained. You can request deletion of your account at any time (see Section 7).

5. Cookies & Local Storage

The site uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits (so you don't have to log in every time). We don't use third-party advertising trackers or cookies.

6. Public Polls & Interactive Quizzes

Our landing page includes optional interactive features like the Style Persona quiz and feature-interest polls. Quiz answers and any name you optionally add to a shareable result card are processed entirely in your browser and are never sent to or stored on our servers. Poll votes are recorded using an anonymous, randomly generated identifier stored in your browser's local storage (not linked to your name, phone number, or account) — this is used only to prevent the same browser from voting twice on the same poll. Poll results themselves (vote totals) are public and visible to anyone who views that poll.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below. If your account has no order or invoice history, we can delete it outright; if it does, we may need to retain certain records for our own legal/accounting obligations even after deactivating your access.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults capable of entering into a contract for custom tailoring services. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
