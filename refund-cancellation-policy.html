Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

✂️ The short version: because every garment is custom-cut and stitched specifically for you, our policy works differently from off-the-shelf retail. Cancel before cutting begins, and you're covered. Once fabric has been cut to your measurements, it can't be resold to anyone else — so refunds beyond that point are limited, but we always stand behind the quality of our stitching.

1. Cancelling Before Work Begins

You may cancel your order any time before cutting/stitching has started, by contacting us directly. Any advance paid will be refunded in full, minus any payment gateway charges already deducted by Razorpay/Paytm on that transaction (a small fee outside our control).

2. Cancelling After Work Has Begun

Once fabric has been cut or stitching has started on your order, the advance amount is non-refundable, since the materials and labor committed to your order cannot be recovered or reused for another customer. You can still discuss adjustments to design or timeline with us directly.

3. Quality Issues & Rework

If your finished garment has a genuine stitching defect or doesn't match the confirmed measurements/specifications, we will rework or alter it free of charge. Please raise any such issue within 7 days of delivery/pickup, along with photos or the garment itself, so we can assess and correct it promptly.

4. What's Not Covered

  • Change of mind after the garment has been correctly stitched to the agreed specifications.
  • Fit issues arising from measurements you provided that later turn out to be inaccurate (we're happy to help with a paid alteration in this case).
  • Natural variation in fabric appearance (color, sheen, print placement) compared to AI-generated design previews — these are illustrative concepts, not exact renders.
